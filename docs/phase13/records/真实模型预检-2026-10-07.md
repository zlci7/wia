Date: 2026-10-07. Baseline revision: `a87c09f`. Provider/model: `deepseek` / `deepseek-v4-flash`. The existing connection was used with isolated data roots. The baseline ran from an archived copy of that revision. The candidate used the same frozen story fixtures and narrative settings, with scene-contract corrections between attempts.

The preflight gate did not pass. No recorded attempt committed a turn. Formal `Service.Execute` remains on its existing per-character pipeline; the centralized candidate remains a test entry. Formal migration and its continuous-play acceptance were not executed.

The original private retry reported the generic `json_schema_invalid` code. A local replay traced it to the nested dialogue contract. The decoder now preserves that business error instead of replacing its field information with a generic outer error.

All six failed attempts retained the opening world clock, turn sequence, message/event heads, scene version, positions, states, item ownership, relationships and personal plans. Failed run records were preserved. This validates failure containment for these samples, not successful scene quality.

There were 16 provider requests. Usage was available for 12: 54,306 input tokens and 45,714 completion tokens, including reasoning. Four timed-out requests had unavailable usage; they are not treated as free. No price or total charge is inferred. No context-capacity failure was observed in this batch.

The first scene responses for the Martin input took 41.737 s and 52.103 s. Their reported reasoning usage was 6,396 and 9,134 tokens. The remaining shared deadline was insufficient for their full corrections. These measurements locate the observed delay; they do not establish why the model chose that much reasoning or predict another provider's latency.

## Delivered corrections

- Fixed-rule input fields keep their program-parsed values. Ordinary elapsed dialogue time belongs to scene offsets and total elapsed minutes.
- The scene context identifies the world assessment already selected at the opening snapshot and supplies its authorized frozen body. Future-only materials still use the shared supplement allowance.
- World progress metadata exposes scheduling state; private plan content remains in the selected owner's context.
- The scene prompt explicitly names player intent enums, mandatory empty fields and kind-specific dialogue/projection rules.
- Nested business validation preserves its local field and expected contract. Correction diagnostics use schema information rather than invalid model values.

The [opt-in test](../../../backend/internal/app/phase13_model_test.go) records requests, diagnostics and before/after snapshots in a separate evidence directory. Completed steps can resume; a failed input allows at most one explicit technical retry. Subsequent retries preserve the original input identity and world baseline. Broad regression commands skip paid tests unless their dedicated configuration variable is set.
